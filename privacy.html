Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

The short version

  • We collect your email, name, password (scrambled), public wallet address and payment records. That's about it.
  • We never ask for, see or store your seed phrase or private keys. Trades and payments are approved in your own wallet.
  • Luna's answers come from Claude, an AI by Anthropic. Your questions to her go through our server to Anthropic to be answered. We don't store them.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't use advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account yourself, anytime, from the account menu.

Luna, the AI

Luna's answers come from Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. When you ask her something, your question, your recent conversation with her and the data she looks up to answer it (for example your public wallet balances, your recent on-chain trades or a coin's market data) are sent from our server to Anthropic, which returns the answer. We don't store your questions or her answers. We do count how many questions each account asks each day and what they cost to answer, to keep usage fair, and delete those counts after 120 days.

If that service isn't available, Luna answers with a smaller AI model that is downloaded to your device and runs there, and those questions stay on your device. Your conversation with Luna is kept only in the open page and is gone when you close or reload it. To answer some questions she may also look up public data, such as coin prices, the weather or a Wikipedia article, straight from the services listed below.

How we use it

  • To provide Lunex: your account, dashboard, wallet view, alerts and Pro features
  • To take and check payments, and to keep the records the law requires
  • To keep Lunex secure, for example by limiting repeated sign-in attempts
  • To answer you when you contact us
  • To send the weekly recap email, if it's switched on. It's on by default for new accounts. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can switch it off any time in Account settings.
  • To show site-wide totals, such as how many launches Lunex has scanned. These are counts, not personal data.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, we don't share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we don't use it to build advertising profiles.

How long we keep it

  • Account data, synced watchlists and practice, and your community posts: until you delete them or your account. Deleting your account deletes your posts too.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Luna usage counts (how many questions you asked each day and what they cost): 120 days. If you delete your account, they are unlinked from it and kept only as anonymous daily totals until then.
  • Payment records (amount, date, method, transaction or invoice ID): kept after an account is deleted, without your email or name, for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Blockchain transactions are public and permanent. Nobody, including us, can delete them.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: open Account settings (from the account menu in the dashboard) and choose Delete account. It takes effect immediately, and any card subscription is cancelled first.
  • See or correct your data: your email, plan and wallet are shown in Account settings, and you can change your wallet there. Account settings also has a download of the data we hold about you, or you can email us.
  • Cancel a card subscription: use Manage billing in the account menu.

Depending on where you live, including California and other US states with privacy laws, you may have the right to know what we hold, get a copy, correct it, delete it, and opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. We don't sell data or run targeted advertising. To use any of these rights, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from your account's email address. We'll answer within 45 days and won't treat you differently for asking. If we turn down a request, you can reply to appeal.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, sign-in sessions are stored only as hashes, the session cookie can't be read by page scripts, and the site uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Age

Lunex is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.
