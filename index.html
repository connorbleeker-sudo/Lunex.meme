Armor.

Where does the data come from?

The radar reads DexScreener. The launch bay streams pump.fun via PumpPortal. Wallets and the vault read the Solana blockchain through the local server's read-only relay. The X feed uses whichever API key you connect.

What does the constellation show?

Horizontal position is the safety score and vertical position is momentum. Star size is volume. The top-right corner, hot and solid, is where the rare good setups live.

Why can a feed be empty?

A provider can be rate limited, not connected yet, or simply waiting for the next event. An empty view doesn't mean nothing is happening.

Can Lunex trade for me?

No. It watches, scores and alerts. You place the trades. Scores are not price forecasts, and most new memecoins go to zero.

How scoring works

Momentum (0–100): 1h buy/sell ratio, 1h volume compared to liquidity, 1h and 6h price change, trade count.

Safety (0–100): starts at 100. Points come off for thin liquidity, very new pairs, market cap far above liquidity, no sells (possible honeypot), no socials, parabolic pumps, and dumps.

Hot and safe is rare. Hot and unsafe is usually a pump that's about to dump.

Real-time mode (optional)

Without a key, wallets are checked on-chain every 30 seconds, which works fine. With a key, trades show up the instant they happen and dev-sells get flagged. To get one: generate a key at pumpportal.fun and send 0.02 SOL to the wallet it creates. Use a throwaway amount, and store the private key it shows you somewhere safe. Never paste that private key here. The key is saved only in this browser.

Where to find good wallets

Pick wallets by their on-chain record, not by follower count. Many big X accounts get paid to shill and sell into their own followers.

Leaderboards that rank wallets by real profit: kolscan.io, gmgn.ai (Smart Money / KOL tabs), cielo.finance. Copy the addresses of wallets with consistent 30-day profits and plenty of trades, not one lucky hit.

Copy-trading has a catch. By the time you see their buy, you're buying after them, and some KOLs dump on the copiers.
