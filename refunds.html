Refund Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

The short version

  • Payments are final. We don't give refunds for Pro time, including partly used time.
  • Cancel anytime. Cancelling a card plan stops future charges, and you keep Pro until the end of the time you've paid for.
  • Crypto passes never renew by themselves, so there's nothing to cancel.

Crypto passes (USDC or SOL)

A crypto pass gives Pro for the number of days shown at checkout, starting when the payment is confirmed on the Solana blockchain. If you already have Pro, the days are added on top. Passes don't renew and you're never charged again unless you choose to pay. Because blockchain payments can't be reversed, crypto payments are final.

Card plans

Card plans renew automatically each week or month, depending on the plan you picked, at the price shown when you subscribed. You can cancel anytime:

  • Open the account menu in the dashboard and choose Manage billing, then cancel the plan.
  • Or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we'll cancel it for you.

Once cancelled, you won't be charged again and Pro stays on until the end of the period you've already paid for. Card payments already made are final. Deleting your account also cancels your card plan straight away.

When we do return money

  • Charged twice by mistake for the same period, or charged after you cancelled: we refund the extra charge.
  • Paid but Pro didn't switch on and we can't fix it: we refund the payment.
  • Overpaid in crypto (sent more than the price shown): we can return the difference to the sending wallet, minus network fees.
  • We close Lunex, or close your account without you breaking the Terms: we refund the unused part of your paid Pro time.
  • The law requires it where you live.

To ask about any of these, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} within 30 days of the payment, from your account's email address, with the date and amount and, for crypto, the transaction ID. Card refunds go back to the same card. Crypto refunds are sent in the same currency to the wallet that paid.

Trades are not covered

This policy covers Lunex Pro payments only. Trades you make, and network or third-party fees, happen on the blockchain and can't be refunded by Lunex. Lunex shows signals, not financial advice.
