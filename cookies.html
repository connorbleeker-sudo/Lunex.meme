Cookie Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

The short version

  • Lunex sets one cookie, and only to keep you signed in.
  • No advertising cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels.

The cookie we set

NameWhat it doesHow long
lunex_sessionKeeps you signed in. It holds a random code, not your details, and page scripts can't read it. Lunex can't work without it, so it's strictly necessary.30 days, or until you sign out

Saved in your browser

Lunex also uses your browser's local storage to remember things on your device, such as your watchlists, alert settings, tracked wallets, your public wallet address, API keys you add, and Luna's downloaded AI model. Watchlists, alerts, tracked wallets and your practice portfolio are also saved to your account so they follow you to other devices; API keys and the AI model stay on your device only. None of it is used for tracking. You can clear it anytime in your browser's settings. Clearing it removes those settings but doesn't delete your account.

Outside services

  • Google Sign-In loads on the sign-in page. Google may set or read its own cookies there, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout and billing pages, mainly to prevent fraud, under Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts and code libraries load from outside servers, which see your IP address. They don't set cookies on Lunex.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. If you block lunex_session, you won't be able to stay signed in. Because Lunex only uses necessary cookies, there's no cookie banner to accept. If we ever add optional cookies, we'll ask first.
